MusicDNA provenance and public-export exclusions
================================================
Upstream: https://github.com/harjot-oberai/MusicDNA
License: https://github.com/harjot-oberai/MusicDNA/blob/master/LICENSE.md
Upstream identifies its license as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

The private radio candidate contained MusicDnaPortScenes.kt, whose source
comment identifies an adaptation of HomeActivity.updatePoints. Earlier notices
incorrectly described all DNA scenes as original. That adapted spiral renderer
is EXCLUDED from this public snapshot, rather than relicensed as Apache-2.0.
The old DNA_SPIRAL preference identifier now falls back to the Apollo helix.

DnaBeatScenes.kt describes the retained HELIX and STRAND geometry as original
Apollo implementations inspired by the general waveform aesthetic, not ports.
SourcedVisualizerScenes.kt similarly identifies the retained circle as original
geometry. Upstream attribution is retained for aesthetic inspiration, not as a
claim of endorsement. No MusicDNA source or spiral port is included here.
